# CAPÍTULO III: REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION

## 3.1. User Stories

> Nota de modelado: los nombres en PascalCase (p. ej. `MealLogged`, `DailyGoalReached`, `BodyMetricsRecorded`) corresponden a eventos de dominio realmente publicados por el sistema. Las notificaciones descritas se presentan **in-app** (el sistema no envía push notifications). Los proveedores externos empleados son **USDA FoodData Central**, **Gemini**, **DeepSeek**, **OpenWeatherMap**, **Stripe** y **Google Health**.

| **EP01** | Behavioral Consistency | As a user, I want the system to monitor my nutritional adherence over time so that it can detect when I am at risk of losing consistency and act before I abandon my plan. | | |
| US01 | Maintain Daily Nutritional Adherence | As a user with physical goals, I want the system to evaluate whether I met my daily nutritional targets so that my adherence status is always up to date and reflects my real consistency. | *Scenario 1: AdherenceStatus becomes ON_TRACK after a successful day* <br><br> Given the user has logged meals and the day's consumed calories reach the daily target <br> When the daily evaluation runs on meal logging (`DailyGoalReached`) <br> Then the system registers a successful day: increments the streak by one, resets consecutive misses to zero, and sets AdherenceStatus to ON_TRACK <br><br> *Scenario 2: A missed day updates the adherence status* <br><br> Given a day is registered as not meeting the daily target <br> When BehavioralProgress is updated <br> Then consecutive misses increase by one; AdherenceStatus becomes AT_RISK from the first miss and DROPPED once misses reach three <br><br> *Scenario 3: Adherence status is surfaced in-app* <br><br> Given the adherence status changes <br> When the user opens the app <br> Then the system surfaces the current AdherenceStatus, streak, and consecutive misses in-app | EP01 |

| US04 | Recover Nutritional Consistency After a Drop | As a user who has been off track, I want the system to recognize when I resume meeting my targets so that my adherence progress is updated and I get positive reinforcement. | *Scenario 1: Adherence returns to ON_TRACK on a successful day* <br><br> Given AdherenceStatus is AT_RISK or DROPPED <br> When the user registers a day that meets the daily target <br> Then AdherenceStatus returns to ON_TRACK, consecutive misses reset to zero, and the return is surfaced in-app <br><br> *Scenario 2: Adherence history reflects the recovery* <br><br> Given the recovery is registered <br> When Analytics processes the update <br> Then the adherence history and dashboard reflect the recovery via event-driven refresh | EP01 |
